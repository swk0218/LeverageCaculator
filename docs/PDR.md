# Product Design Requirements

The primary task begins immediately below a compact header; there is no marketing hero. The information order is product selection, purchase rows, live purchase summary, current-price control, calculate action, four key results, detailed comparison, methodology, FAQ, ads, and footer.

Astryx neutral supplies accessible React controls and baseline tokens. Local overrides use a warm paper background, near-black ink, yellow accent, Korean red for gains, Korean blue for losses, 8px rhythm, crisp 1–2px borders, and hard offset shadows. Long text and financial numbers use a readable Korean sans-serif stack; pixel styling is limited to the wordmark and small decorative marks.

Mobile uses one column and stacked purchase fields. Desktop centers a 760px calculator within a maximum 1120px page. Touch targets are at least 44px and meaning never depends on color alone.
